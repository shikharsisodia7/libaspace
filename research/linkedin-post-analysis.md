## Michael Yan / Simplify: "POV: job searching in 2026"

- Direct URL: <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/myan_pov-job-searching-in-2026-ps-i-built-activity-7440515292029677568-16ry>
- Format: native image / meme with a short product note.
- Public signal: the accessible page showed a visible comment thread with candidates recognizing the entry-level search experience. No total-reaction claim is made here.
- Audience and pain: early-career candidates facing an exhausting, high-pressure search.
- Why it works: the POV framing creates quick identification. The humor lowers the cost of commenting, and the Simplify mention appears after the shared recognition rather than before it.
- Save / share mechanism: it is lightweight and easy to send to a peer, though less saveable than a practical checklist.
- JobNova lesson: recognition can earn attention, but JobNova should add a useful action so the product bridge is stronger than a joke.

## Adam Biddlecombe: "Most people spend months looking for a job"

- Direct URL: <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/adam-bidd_most-people-spend-months-looking-for-a-job-activity-7201613287837368320-gpU1>
- Format: resource-list visual and text.
- Public signal: LinkedIn displayed 238 reactions and 81 comments when checked on August 13, 2026.
- Audience and pain: job seekers trying to reduce a multi-tool, repetitive workflow.
- Why it works: readers can scan a task-oriented list quickly, save it for later, or share it with someone job hunting. The strong hook creates attention, but the categorized utility is the durable engagement mechanism.
- JobNova lesson: deliver immediate utility before a call to action. Do not copy the post's seven-day hiring promise.

## Amy Chick: "Unpopular opinion: I do not find Teal to be a helpful tool"

- Direct URL: <https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amychick_unpopular-opinion-i-dont-find-teal-to-be-activity-7176976601174167552-Ckk1>
- Format: text-led contrarian analysis of an AI job-search tool.
- Public signal: LinkedIn displayed 125 comments when checked on August 13, 2026.
- Audience and pain: job seekers evaluating whether customization tools save meaningful time or add work.
- Why it works: the contrarian hook makes the post commentable, while the nuance makes it credible. It frames a genuine tradeoff rather than offering a simple endorsement, so readers can contribute their own workflow experience.
- JobNova lesson: showing the role of human judgment makes automation messaging more trustworthy.

## Pattern applied
