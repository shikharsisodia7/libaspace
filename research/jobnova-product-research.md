## Claim discipline

- **Verified public product statement:** JobNova's public site displayed the named feature descriptions when reviewed.
- **Company marketing claim:** JobNova's figures for manual-search time, recruiter visibility, monthly application volume, career-page scale, comparative uniqueness, testimonials, and timing claims are not independently validated here.
- **Strategic inference:** company-page monitoring may be useful to candidates who feel trapped in crowded, habitual feeds. That is a hypothesis to test, not proof that a career-page listing is always earlier or better.

The affiliate program is deliberately not a primary recommendation. A student-facing campaign should earn trust through career utility first.
