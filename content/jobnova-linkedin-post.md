# Original LinkedIn post - JobNova

Most students are searching for internships in the same places, then wondering why every listing already feels crowded.

That is not a reason to abandon LinkedIn, Handshake, or job boards. It is a reason to treat them as part of a system, not the whole system.

A more useful weekly routine:

- Define the roles, locations, and companies you actually want.
- Watch company career pages for fresh openings.
- Keep one clean source of truth for applications and deadlines.
- Spend the saved time on the parts a tool cannot do for you: a strong portfolio, interview practice, and thoughtful outreach.

The hard part of an early-career search is rarely a lack of effort. It is directing effort toward the right opportunities early enough.

JobNova is built around that workflow: it says it monitors company career pages, matches roles to your criteria, and can handle repetitive application steps using the information you provide. The important part is still your judgment: set thoughtful criteria and review what represents you.

If you are recruiting for internships or new-grad roles this year, what is the most repetitive part of your process?
